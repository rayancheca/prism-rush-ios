## 1. Mandate

I was hired to find out whether this thing is actually done, because the man who built it says it is
and his own documentation says a lot of things. I do not get paid for telling him it is
well-architected. It is well-architected — the simulation core is genuinely excellent, the seam is
clean, 178 tests pass in nine seconds — and none of that is what I was asked. I was asked whether a
player can reach, use, and finish every feature this project claims to have, and the answer is no.
The gaps here are not textual: there is not one `TODO`, `FIXME`, `HACK`, `XXX`, or `fatalError` in
95 files, and I verified that rather than assuming it. The gaps are semantic, and they cluster with
horrible consistency in one place: **the moment a surface has to say something other than "here is
your happy path."** Empty boards, unaffordable prices, unloaded stores, signed-out services, states
that exist for one frame after a purchase — that is where this app stops being finished. I also
killed four of my own findings and five of session 001's, because a contractor who invoices for
forty gaps and can only defend twelve does not get hired twice.

## 2. What I examined, and what I deliberately did not

**Examined.** All 26 files in `UI/`, both `IAP/` files plus `Products.storekit`, all seven `Meta/`
files, `Core/GameCore.swift` and `Tuning.swift`, `Audio/SynthEngine.swift`, `Services/`,
`Store/metadata.md`, `README.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, both ship docs, and session 001's 108-row claims
ledger. Then — before writing anything — I built the app and ran it: 14 screens, 6 launch-hook
states, 4 clean installs, ~30 screenshots, every one of which I actually opened.

**Deliberately not.**
- *Game feel, difficulty, economy balance, session shape.* That is AUDIT-002 (session 003) and
  duplicating it destroys the independence the program is built on. Where I noticed something
  (the ×5 multiplier is trivially reached; world 13 is world 1 recoloured) I flagged it for that
  session rather than scoring it.
- *App Store compliance.* AUDIT-003's. I tripped over two things — no Privacy Policy URL anywhere,
  and `Store/metadata.md` selling a three-world game — and I have recorded them and moved on.
- *Concurrency, actor isolation, retain cycles, pooling.* AUDIT-006's.
- *`GameView.swift`'s 1,224 lines.* Tracked as PR-0241, not mine.
- *Renumbering or merging session 001's backlog.* Session 009 triages. Where I think an item is
  wrong I say so in §5 and propose `WONTFIX`; I removed nothing.
- *Anything needing hardware.* Listed in §6.

## 3. Findings

Filed in full in `03_BACKLOG.md` as **PR-0300 … PR-0323**. Ranked here by severity. Every one
survived at least one hostile verifier whose explicit job was to refute it, and each cites
`file:line`. `[RUNTIME]` means I saw it on a running build rather than inferring it.

### SEV1

**PR-0300 · Cold launch adopts the cloud profile wholesale and discards the local one.**
`ProfileStore` has a `merged()` function that exists precisely to prevent this, and the cold-launch
path never calls it. A player who plays offline on device A and then launches on device B loses the
offline session. Verifier note: "SURVIVES (SEV1, borders SEV0)." Overlaps session 001's PR-0005 —
**session 009 should merge these**; I file it because the mechanism (merge not called *on that
path*) is more specific than PR-0005 states.

**PR-0301 · No Privacy Policy URL exists anywhere, and neither ship doc asks for one.**
The app has Sign in with Apple, Game Center and iCloud KVS. Not in `project.yml`, not in
`Store/metadata.md`, not in `SHIP_CHECKLIST.md`, not in `APP_STORE_SETUP.md`. This is AUDIT-003's to
score against the live guidelines; it is filed here because it is a *missing artifact*, which is
squarely my mandate.

### SEV2

**PR-0302 · [RUNTIME] The Mystery Box's OPEN button is inert when you cannot afford it, and the app
already knows how to do this properly.** At 100 coins, `OPEN · 300` renders as a full-saturation
cyan→magenta CTA. Tapping it does nothing at all — no shortfall copy, no route, no shake, no toast.
The same app handles the identical case correctly twice: the Worlds `UnlockPanel` shows
`NEED 400 MORE` + `GET COINS`, and `GameOverView`'s revive shows `NEED 46 MORE  150` on a dimmed
pill. The pattern exists and is good; the Mystery Box does not use it. Decrees 3 and 4.

**PR-0303 · [RUNTIME] The Mystery Box overlay has no backdrop scrim, so the odds table — the one
surface that exists for honesty — is the least legible thing on screen.** Verified settled across
two identical consecutive frames, not mid-animation. `BALANCED PICK`, `7,000`, `$4.99`,
`FIRST PURCHASE +50%` ×2, `+32% BONUS`, `BEST VALUE`, `16,000` and `40,000` all read straight
through the odds rows and the OPEN/CLOSE controls. `CLOSE` is also bare text with no button chrome,
unlike every other control in the app. Decree 6.

**PR-0304 · [RUNTIME] The Missions board says "ALL CLEAR" on first launch, when every row is 0/N.**
`ALL CLEAR · NEW BOARD IN 3:32` sits above seven rows reading `0/150 … 0/30`. It is true only in the
sense that nothing is *claimable*; it reads as "you finished everything", which on the single most
important state this screen has is exactly backwards. Same screen also shows `RESETS 3:32` (no unit)
next to `RESETS 3D`. Decree 3.

**PR-0305 · [RUNTIME] Once muted, a player cannot unmute from the hub or from Settings, and mute
survives relaunch.** `toggleMute()` (`GameView.swift:603`) has exactly one caller —
`GameView.swift:1072`, the in-run/game-over corner control — and it persists to the profile
(`:606`) and is restored at launch (`:200-201`). Settings has three volume sliders and no mute
control. To restore audio a muted player must start a run and find the corner speaker.

**PR-0306 · [RUNTIME] In the pre-approval store state, seven hardcoded USD prices render at full
opacity and every one of them is inert.** This **re-scores session 001's PR-0290 and refutes its
"money bug" framing** — see §5. Nothing is charged, because tapping `$0.99` does nothing whatsoever:
no toast, no sheet, no error. The `.loading` state shimmers its price pills and `.offline` shows a
first-viewport RETRY card; `.notConfigured` — the state a pre-approval build actually sits in — has
neither, and its only disclosure is a grey footnote roughly six screens below the first price.
Decrees 3 and 4.

**PR-0307 · Post-revive play is invisible to every mission, achievement and XP award, while the same
play still moves the numbers on the Profile screen.** Two verifiers independently promoted this
(SEV3 → SEV2). A player who pays 150 coins to continue gets a run that counts for their stats and
not for their progression. Decree 5 — the advertised bonus is not fully delivered.

**PR-0308 · Restore Purchases reports "Purchases restored." to a player who has never bought
anything.** Found independently by three of the ten finders. The failure path additionally leaks a
raw error string into a different screen.

**PR-0309 · Sign in with Apple completes successfully and changes nothing observable.** The card
promises to "secure your account across devices". Nothing in the app reads the resulting identity;
there is no signed-in state that differs from signed-out. Combined with PR-0301 and the absence of
any account-deletion path, this is the weakest feature in the app relative to what it claims.

**PR-0310 · The daily Game Center leaderboard is advertised in four documents and has no in-app
viewer.** `prismrush.daily` is submitted to and never displayed.

**PR-0311 · [RUNTIME] The Game Center row on Profile is a dead card that tells the player to quit
the app.** `Leaderboards need Game Center / Sign in from the Settings app, then relaunch.` — no tap
target, no in-app sign-in, and the instruction asks the player to leave. Decree 4.

**PR-0312 · Every character swatch crops the top off the character.** 18 of 20 crests and every
antenna tip are cut off in the grid, shop and next-unlock swatches; the legendary aura ring is
structurally wider than its own canvas and is clipped on *every* surface including the menu hero and
the splash. Decree 2 — the previews are not showing the character you get.

**PR-0313 · Fourteen `PR_*` launch hooks compile into the Release binary with zero `#if DEBUG`
gating.** I verified the gating claim myself: `grep -rn "#if DEBUG" PrismRush --include='*.swift'`
returns **0** across all 95 files, against 17 `ProcessInfo` reads of `PR_*`. One of these hooks
destructively rewrites the saved profile. Exploitability is AUDIT-007's to price — as a completeness
matter, debug affordances are shipping in the release path.

**PR-0314 · Audio-engine start failure is permanent — every recovery path is gated behind the flag
the failure clears.** One failed start and the app is silent for the rest of its life, with no
signal to the player.

**PR-0315 · Game Center scores are silently discarded for the entire session when authentication
fails at launch.** No retry, no queue. A player who launches in a tunnel loses every score that
session and is never told.

**PR-0316 · Five products simultaneously advertise the single first-purchase bonus.** All four coin
packs carry `FIRST PURCHASE +50%` and the Starter Bundle carries `FIRST PURCHASE OFFER`. Exactly one
can ever be true. Honest in mechanism, misleading as presented — the badge reads as a per-pack
property. Decree 5.

**PR-0317 · Two icon systems for the same five power-ups.** The shop, the reward burst and the hub
still draw SF Symbols while the in-run surfaces use the procedural `PowerUpGlyph`. The same power-up
is a different picture depending on where you meet it.

**PR-0318 · Achievement-gated characters require a CLAIM tap the requirement copy never mentions.**
The progress bar reaches 100% and the skin stays locked, with nothing on screen explaining why.

## 4. The three things that worry me most

**1. Every failure state in this app is unfinished, and they all fail the same way.**
This is not a list of unrelated bugs. Ask any surface "what do you show when the normal thing has
not happened?" and it answers badly: the store not loaded (PR-0306), not enough coins in the gacha
(PR-0302), a board with nothing done (PR-0304), signed out of Game Center (PR-0311, PR-0315), audio
failed to start (PR-0314), nothing to restore (PR-0308), a purchase awaiting a parent's approval
(rendered as a **red error**). The happy path is polished to a shine and everything beside it is
either raw, silent, or lying. That is the signature of a project that was declared finished from the
inside, by someone who had already unlocked everything. It is also, mercifully, the cheapest class
of defect to fix — and the app *already contains the correct pattern* in the Worlds unlock panel and
the revive offer. Most of §3 is "use the pattern you already wrote."

**2. Twenty-five of fifty-nine user-facing features have no automated test of any kind, and the
green build actively conceals it.** `swift test -c release` prints 178 passing tests in nine seconds
and compiles **none** of `UI/`, `Render/`, `IAP/`, `SynthEngine`, StoreKit or GameKit.
`CharacterParityTests.swift` is wrapped in `#if canImport(UIKit)` and silently compiles to nothing
there. The entire interactive surface is covered by six XCUITests in one file. Every finding in §3
lives in exactly the region the test suite cannot see, and that is not a coincidence — it is the
mechanism. A future session that trusts a green `swift test` for a UI change will ship a regression
and be told everything is fine.

**3. The documentation is not merely stale, it is confidently wrong in the files agents read first.**
`CLAUDE.md` — the first file every session loads — is wrong on four load-bearing facts, including
the test count. `Store/metadata.md` sells a three-world game and the ship docs instruct the owner to
paste it verbatim into App Store Connect. The README claims twelve bespoke skies (nine exist) and
per-world music that an explicit owner decree deliberately disabled. This program's entire premise
is that written memory survives and context does not; a chain of sessions inheriting confidently
wrong memory will compound the error rather than catch it. **PR-0010 must land before anything else
in the docs, and the ledger in §7 exists to make every rewritten claim checkable.**
